---
name: merchant-change
description: "Investigate a merchant's explanation for increased activity and compare it with independent disposition records when no repeated-authorization explanation applies."
---

Check transaction coverage and repeated authorization first. If a repeated group is present, use alert-investigation rather than applying merchant corroboration to override settlement evidence.
Read merchant originals and independently authored dispositions. A merchant claim by itself is insufficient corroboration. An empty case-bound catalog establishes a missing record only within its scope and cutoff.
Preserve both positive and negative evidence; distinguish confirmed change, contradicted explanation and missing material.
