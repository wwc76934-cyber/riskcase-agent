---
name: alert-investigation
description: "Investigate payment alert groups when repeated authorizations, settlement counts or incomplete transaction history may explain the alert."
---

Read the bound transaction overview and applicable procedure before expanding the investigation.
For repeated authorization, reconcile the repeated group with source settlement records; the group's debit count does not describe every transaction in the overview.
If history is incomplete, retain that gap even if merchant documents sound reassuring. Stop when required evidence is missing or sufficient for the declared research policy.
Do not infer a currency, a fraud verdict or an account action from a score.
For overlapping explanations, read [precedence](references/precedence.md).
